# Ideation

## Problem
Campus administration (attendance, leave, certificates, fees, lab seats, library clearance) runs on paper registers, WhatsApp messages and spreadsheets:
- Students don't know where their leave application is, or why it was rejected.
- Staff re-enter the same information, and nobody can answer "who is outside campus right now?".
- Principals get interrupted for every approval, and there's no record of who decided what.
- Generic ERP dashboards show *everything* at once, so people ignore them.

## Users
Students · Teachers (Usthads) · Academic Assistant · Librarian · Lab Charge · Principal / Vice Principal · Office (Admin).

## Solution: OURCAMPUS
> *Complexity belongs inside the system, not on the user's screen.*

1. **Task-first home screens.** Each person sees only what needs them today ("2 leave applications waiting for your decision"). Each item opens the exact record.
2. **One workflow per real process.** Leave: *Student applies → Principal/VP decides → Academic Assistant clears*, with a live tracker for the student.
3. **Positions, not portals.** A teacher who is also the Librarian gets library tasks inside the same Staff Portal.
4. **Explicit records.** Attendance is never assumed: an approved leave doesn't silently mark someone absent or "on leave".
5. **Friendly, not gamified.** Rounded, colourful, calm UI, with no streaks or points that pressure students.

## Success measures
- A student applies for leave in under a minute and always knows its status.
- Staff finish daily attendance in under two minutes per class.
- Approvals have an audit trail (who, when, note).
- No user can see or change data outside their role (enforced in the database).
